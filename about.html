ABOUT LIMLODGE

A little help
finding your place.

LIMLODGE is a student accommodation website concept made to help university students explore room options around Sierra Leone.

WHO WE ARE

Built around
student needs.

We are the team behind LIMLODGE, a student project that brings accommodation examples together in one easy-to-browse place. The idea grew from a familiar challenge: finding a room that works for your studies, budget and daily journey can take a lot of time.

Our purpose is to make it easier to explore and compare the basics before speaking directly with a landlord or accommodation provider.

Explore student rooms ↗
THE PROBLEM WE WANT TO HELP WITH

Less searching
in scattered places.

Students may have to compare room prices, locations, transport, room sharing and facilities from different sources. It can be hard to tell which details matter or what questions to ask. LIMLODGE groups those details in a consistent format to help students make a clearer shortlist.

MISSION & VISION

A clearer start
to campus life.

Our mission is to give students a simple way to explore accommodation options near their university and compare the details that affect everyday life.

Our vision is for students to feel better informed as they look for a safe, suitable place to live while studying.

OPTIONS TO EXPLORE

Different rooms
for different needs.

Students can browse standard single rooms, self-contained rooms and shared rooms. Listings include example rent, location and facilities so you can consider what fits your plans and budget.

To use the website, search by town, neighbourhood, university or room name. Filter by room type, budget or a facility such as a study desk, then open a listing to review its details. Compare a few options and confirm all information with the provider before making arrangements.
